## Text and maths

Paragraphs are Markdown: **bold**, *italic*, `code`, [links](#/w99-example). Inline maths is $\hat y = \theta x$; display maths goes between `$$` lines:

$$
\SSE(\theta) = \sum_{j=1}^{m} (y_j - \theta x_j)^2.
$$

Shared macros such as `\SSE`, `\bx` and `\T` are defined in `src/components/Math.tsx`. Write a literal dollar sign as `\$`, and `\<`, `\{` for `<` and `{` in text.

## A step-by-step derivation

<Steps>
Optional text before the first step.

<Step title="Differentiate">
$$
\frac{d}{d\theta}\SSE = -2\sum_j x_j (y_j - \theta x_j)
$$
</Step>

<Step title="Set to zero and solve">
$$
\hat\theta = \frac{\sum_j x_j y_j}{\sum_j x_j^2}
$$
</Step>
</Steps>
