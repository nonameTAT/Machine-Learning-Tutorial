<Problem id="A1" title="Fit through the origin" lesson="example">

Derive the least-squares slope $\hat\theta$ for $\hat y = \theta x$.

<Hint>Differentiate the sum of squared errors and set it to zero.</Hint>

<Solution>
$$
\frac{d}{d\theta}\sum_j (y_j - \theta x_j)^2 = -2\sum_j x_j(y_j - \theta x_j) = 0 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \hat\theta = \frac{\sum_j x_jy_j}{\sum_j x_j^2}.
$$
</Solution>
